{\centering \large\bfseries Abstract\par}
\vspace{1cm}

\begin{adjustwidth}{0.1\textwidth}{0.1\textwidth}
  \noindent We present an study of the $\gamma p \to K_S^0 K_S^0 p$ channel measured by the GlueX experiment. This channel selects for resonances with $J^{PC} = \text{even}^{++}$ quantum numbers (the $f_0$, $f_2$, $a_0$, and $a_2$ light, flavorless mesons) and potentially contains contributions from the lightest scalar glueball ($G_0$). Data from the 2017, 2018, and 2020 run periods are used to perform a mass-independent (binned) partial-wave analysis to distinguish spin states. Due to the polarized photon beam at GlueX, we are also able to extract reflectivity amplitudes for the first time in this channel. Additionally, we perform mass-dependent partial-wave analyses using a fixed $K$-matrix parameterization to attempt a determination of the relative contribution of each resonance, and we present a guided initialization method which facilitates navigation through a parameter space with many local minima. These mass-dependent fits are a proof-of-concept in using fixed $K$-matrix models constrained by other experiments to measure photoproduction amplitudes for overlapping states which may only differ by isospin, a property which cannot be distinguished in this channel.
\end{adjustwidth}
